Frequently asked questions

Which Odoo versions are supported?

Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise.

Does it change my data or business logic?

No. It only adds styling, a sidebar and a home dashboard. Your data, models and workflows are untouched.

How does it use my company logo and colours?

The logo is shown in the sidebar. Theme colours come from Settings → Companies → Document Layout colours, or are detected from your logo.

Where did the top menu go?

On desktop every app and its child menus live in the left sidebar, so the top bar stays clean. On phones the standard menu is kept.

Can I go back to the standard look?

Yes. Uninstall the module and the default home action and look are restored.

Who sees which numbers on the dashboard?

Each user sees only what their access rights and record rules allow; blocks for apps that are not installed are hidden.

✅ Good to know

  • Depends only on Web and Discuss; Sales, Invoicing, Purchase, Project and Calendar blocks appear automatically when installed.
  • All figures respect each user’s access rights and record rules.
  • The dashboard becomes the home page for internal users (restored on uninstall).
  • Brand colours come from Settings → Companies → Document Layout colours, or are detected from the company logo.
