🚨 Why This Module

Negative stock is not just an accounting anomaly — it's a signal of broken processes. Unfulfilled orders, phantom inventory, inaccurate financials, and angry customers all stem from the same root cause: no guardrails at the point of delivery.

  • 📉 Phantom Inventory Ruins Reporting
    On-hand balances become unreliable and distort finance decisions.
  • ⚖️ Audit & Compliance Risk
    Every override is logged with reason, timestamp, and user.
  • 🏭 Warehouse-Specific Rules
    Configure policies per warehouse, category, or product.
  • 🚨 Real-Time Prevention
    Stops negative stock at validation — not after damage is done.

⚙️ Real-Time Intervention Engine

  1. Transfer Initiated – System detects potential negative stock.
  2. Policy Engine Fires – Product → Category → Warehouse → Global.
  3. Action Triggered – Allow / Warn / Block.
  4. Override With Reason – Logged permanently.
  5. Chatter & Email Alerts – Automatic notifications.
  6. Dashboard Updated – Live monitoring view refreshed.
